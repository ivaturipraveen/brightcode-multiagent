01 · Strategic Overview

The WoW Thesis

WoW has been conceived not as a restaurant project, but as an operational response to a structural inefficiency within the global premium dining industry. Traditional fine dining creates cultural value but rarely produces replicable economic value.

WoW replicates processes — not chefs. Scales infrastructure — not restaurants. Monetizes supply, certification, and IP — not tables.

"WoW is an IP company disguised as hospitality."

02 · Business Model

Revenue Architecture —
Four Platform Layers

01
Layer 1 · Flagship & Experience Units

Directly controlled locations whose role is strategic, not financial. Each unit: 24 seats · 2 turns/day · 4 operating days · 80% occupancy. Avg blended revenue: €360k–420k/year. Purpose: demonstrate FFD system, validate training, anchor brand narrative.

Avg menu price Italy€35–45
Avg menu price Europe€50
02
Layer 2 · Fast Fine Licensing (FFL)

The primary expansion mechanism. Licensee CAPEX ~€100k vs €350–800k for independent restaurant. WoW monetizes through initial fee + ongoing royalty.

Initial fee (early)€10k → €15k → €25k
Ongoing royalty5% → 7% → 9%
Licensee payback12–18 months
03
Layer 3 · Centralized Supply Margin

Unlike traditional franchising, WoW does not leave sourcing to licensees. Structurally embedded supply flows create recurring revenue independent of restaurant profitability.

Food supply margin+20% on WoW cost
Wine supply margin+30% on WoW cost
Per unit annual platform income€40–50k
04
Layer 4 · Knowledge & Certification (Phase 2)

As network grows, WoW monetizes the codified FFD system through training certification, operational auditing, and continuous format updates. Increases switching costs for operators.

Effective platform capture rate~12% of unit turnover
Total per unit (recurring)~€55k/year

08 · Key Risks & Mitigation

Risk Framework

Execution Risk (Early Stage)
Mitigation: Pilot direct clusters validate the model before scaling. International team structured and ready for scale-up. FFD and FFL systems already engineered and codified.
Licensee Quality & Consistency
Mitigation: Selective licensing, training and certification through flagship units. Operational auditing and QA protocols embedded in the FFD system.
Model & Supply Chain Scalability
Mitigation: Centralized sourcing from Italian producers, controlled distribution by specialists. Hub model scales only when demand is secured.
Competitive Imitation
Mitigation: Proprietary FFD culinary engineering, FFL licensing model, and integrated supply chain create a defensible IP moat. Global trademark in 50+ countries.
Consumer Skepticism
Mitigation: Engineered expectation asymmetry — the WoW effect is by design. Michelin-starred chef equity partnership provides instant credibility anchoring.
Current Stage
Completed de-risking milestones:
  • International corporate structure defined
  • Founding team and ecosystem in place
  • Export–Import corridor already active

  • Culinary protocols codified with Starred Chefs
  • Developers active across 3 regions
Outstanding:
